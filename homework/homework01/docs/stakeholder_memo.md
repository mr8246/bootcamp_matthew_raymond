# Stakeholder Memo

**To:** Individual Investor (Project Stakeholder)
**From:** Matthew Raymond
**Re:** Financial Health Analysis Tool — Project Kickoff

## Summary

This memo outlines a project to help you quickly assess a company's financial health using its quarterly earnings data, without needing to manually dig through dense financial filings.

## The Problem

Right now, earnings data is scattered across inconsistent formats and hard to compare across companies or time periods. With corporate earnings growth running unusually high in 2026 and stock valuations sitting above historical averages, it's more important than ever to separate real financial strength from market hype — but doing that manually takes time you don't have.

## What This Project Will Deliver

A cleaned, easy-to-read summary of key financial health indicators — revenue growth, EPS trends, and profit margins — pulled directly from a company's reported earnings. Instead of reading through full filings, you'll get a clear, comparable snapshot to support your buy/hold/sell decisions.

## Timing

This tool is most useful around earnings season, when new quarterly data becomes available, but can also be checked periodically to track how a company's financial health trends over time.

## Next Steps

Over the coming weeks, this project will move through data collection, cleaning, and analysis stages, with regular updates pushed to the project repository.
